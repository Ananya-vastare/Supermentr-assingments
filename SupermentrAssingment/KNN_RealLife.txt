Assignment Name: KNN in Real Life

Answer:

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) works by finding users with similar preferences and recommending items they liked.

Netflix Example:

    -If User A and User B both like action and thriller movies
    -And User B watched a new movie
    -Then that movie is recommended to User A

Small Similarity Example:

User	Action	Comedy	Romance
A	       5	    2	    1
B	       4	    2	    1
C	       1	    5	    4

Distance(A, B) -> Small ->  Similar
Distance(A, C) ->  Large ->  Not similar

Conclusion:
KNN recommends items based on closest (most similar) users.
