Description: Perform K-Means clustering on a mall dataset and describe customer groups

Answer:

Steps Performed:

Loaded the mall customer dataset (Age, Income, Spending Score)
Selected relevant features (Annual Income & Spending Score)
Applied K-Means clustering algorithm
Used the Elbow Method to find optimal number of clusters (K = 5)
Visualized clusters using scatter plot

Customer Groups Identified:

1) High Income – High Spending
    a)Premium customers
    b)Target for luxury products
2) High Income – Low Spending
    a)Potential customers
    b)Need marketing strategies to increase spending
3)Low Income – High Spending
    a)Impulsive buyers
    b)Offer discounts and deals
4)Low Income – Low Spending
    a)Least valuable segment
    b)Minimal targeting required
5)Average Income – Average Spending
    a)Regular customers
    b)Maintain engagement

Conclusion:
K-Means clustering helps businesses understand customer behavior and target specific groups effectively for marketing and sales strategies.
